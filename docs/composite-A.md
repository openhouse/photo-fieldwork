# Revision A composite

This composite keeps Revision A's dependency-light CLI, exact quota assignment, release-bound catalog plan, receipt integrity, and production-shaped tests as the spine. It imports the strongest compatible contracts surfaced across the `feature/revision-*` branch family without adopting a second lifecycle or a parallel truth model.

## Selected contracts

| Source strength | Composite contract |
| --- | --- |
| A: exact assignment and release identity | Keep the fast deterministic 4,000-item solver and schema-2 release plan. |
| B and C: typed states and explicit gates | Give the release a bounded class and keep human decisions explicit. |
| D and H: adversarial and meta-evaluation | Type eval oracles, require positive controls, and mutation-test the evaluator. |
| E and J: human review provenance | Require an identified human, visible reason, and review round for final decisions. |
| F and G: pixel and source reality | Keep decode verification, source identity, and operationally useful diagnostics. |
| I: exact evidence binding | Bind config and feedback content in evaluation, validation, release, and execution. |
| K: holdout independence | Audit UUID plus duplicate, perceptual, and burst relation leakage. |
| L: privacy and threat boundaries | Keep identifiers out of default holdout reports and publication default closed. |
| M: execution attempt chain | Preserve two distinct complete production plans and receipts before comparing idempotence. |
| N: scale pressure | Keep the 4,000-item overlapping assignment in every eval run. |

## Deliberate boundaries

- Requested role-play is editorial counsel, never eyewitness evidence or release authority.
- A safety HOLD propagates across known image relations before selection.
- Final evaluation requires identified-human decision provenance.
- An editor-field release is not publication clearance.
- Two incomplete receipts cannot prove idempotence, even when their visible counts agree.
- The composite does not add a database, service, cloud dependency, or competing orchestration framework.

## Stop condition

Stop hill climbing when the original failures, adversarial variants, evaluator mutations, positive controls, synthetic end-to-end demo, Python suite, and Swift typecheck pass together without weakening an assertion. Live Photos authorization, editorial quality, consent, rights, and real production writes remain human and environment gates.
