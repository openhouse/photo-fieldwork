## Composite behavior

- The capacity network now satisfies exact view quotas, named-person floors, and person-free floors jointly. It cannot report a false infeasibility caused by a greedy post-selection swap.
- An unresolved safety state propagates transitively through perceptual, duplicate, and burst relations before ranking. Event, person, place, and date relationships do not propagate holds.
- Missing safety state fails closed. `clear_for_editor_field` requires an identified actor with `human-editor` authority.
- Fresh evaluation rows determine precision, coverage, uncertainty, and per-view gates. Regression canaries are measured separately and can block release without improving those estimates.
- Every judgment requires a visible reason. The exact sample hash binds sample role and canary expectations as well as image, view, proposal, and master identity.
- Final-holdout audits reject missing IDs, duplicate IDs, internal related frames, and cross-boundary UUID, perceptual-cluster, duplicate-group, or burst-group leakage.
- Run progress is derived from an owner-only append-only event ledger. Phase events carry regular-file size and SHA-256 receipts, legal order, a hash chain, and compare-and-swap revision. A damaged materialized state can be recovered; changed evidence blocks progress.
- Every PhotoKit plan names the required helper revision and exact source membership. Each launch gets a nonce; the Swift helper verifies source membership before mutation and returns the nonce, helper revision, and SHA-256 of the exact plan bytes it executed.
- Public handoff is a separate allowlisted projection. Only cleared derivative records proceed, and the resulting manifest contains no source UUID, People association, raw OCR, coordinate, or private evidence field.

## Deliberate limits

The composite does not claim that synthetic evals establish visual quality, consent, rights, provenance, live Photos compatibility, or publication approval. It does not automate public clearance, identify faces, upload private evidence, mutate Photos SQLite, or replace the ten-item write test and independent read-only verification.

The local review workbench, broader retrieval indexes, and additional catalog adapters remain valid follow-on work. They were not imported merely to maximize branch coverage.

## Production-shaped benchmark

A five-run local benchmark used 12,000 synthetic candidates, five overlapping views, exact 1,600-item quotas, a 20 percent named-person floor, and a 50 percent person-free floor. The composite selected exactly 8,000 items with view counts of 1,600 each, 3,000 named-person rows, and 5,000 person-free rows. Median solver time was 0.2080 seconds; the observed range was 0.1895 to 0.2240 seconds. The unconstrained ranked assignment met both floors, so no diversity repair flow was needed.

This is a development-machine observation, not a portable performance claim or wall-clock CI threshold.
