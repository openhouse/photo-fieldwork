# Changelog

## 0.2.0 - unreleased

### Evaluation-driven hardening

- Add realistic skill prompts and executable release-contract evals, including a deterministic 4,000-item overlapping assignment.
- Replace first-match assignment with exact capacity-aware assignment and actionable deficit reports.
- Bind catalog plans to frozen source membership, exact master assignments, exact final-evaluation and validation reports, and plan content.
- Recheck receipt artifacts while deriving run state and block later passing phases after evidence drift.
- Preserve the measured deterministic and paired-agent Revision A hill climbs in `evals/iterations/`.

### Added

- Receipt-backed run orchestration with semantic-version reservation and resumable phase state.
- Validated local machine profiles outside Git.
- Whole-visible-library inventory support and operational Photos access probes.
- Executable per-view evaluation, final-field, replacement, event, lineage, and historical-exclusion gates.
- Explainable retrieval diagnostics and conservative sequence clusters.
- Preview decode verification and transparent local preview caching.
- Local review workbench with data-minimized CSV export.
- Compact WAL-aware independent verification evidence.
- Synthetic CI coverage for run state, privacy boundaries, and verification.

### Changed

- The Apple Photos helper is now a generic profile-selected adapter template.
- Completion reports are derived from append-only receipts.
- `exploratory_fraction` and `event_cluster_limit` now affect selection.
- Potentially sensitive `needs-review` items fail closed like HOLD items.

### Safety

- Populated profiles, local paths, live Photos identifiers, pixels, OCR, faces, coordinates, and real manifests remain outside the public repository.
- Publication permission remains outside the meaning of editor-field membership.
